Security Information

What the protection model does and does not cover.

  • At rest: vault contents are encrypted before export.
  • KDF: Argon2id with versioned parameters.
  • Encryption: AES-256-GCM with a fresh 96-bit nonce per export.
  • Salt: unique 32-byte random salt per vault.
  • Key: AES CryptoKey is non-extractable in Web Crypto.
  • Storage: no localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, IndexedDB, or database.
  • Network: application policy blocks external connections.
  • Threat limit: a compromised OS/browser/extensions may observe memory or keystrokes.
